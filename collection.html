TEXTILE HERITAGE

THE FABRIC STORY

Our collection takes inspiration from African textile traditions such as Ankara, Kente, and Mudcloth. These traditions demonstrate how pattern, color, symbolism, and craftsmanship can communicate identity and history.

ANKARA

Ankara is known for vibrant, colorful printed textiles widely associated with African fashion and everyday expressive clothing.

KENTE

Kente is a Ghanaian textile tradition celebrated for its intricate woven geometric patterns, where every color and visual motif carries specific symbolic meaning.

MUDCLOTH

Mudcloth, or Bogolanfini, is a traditional Malian textile art recognized for hand-dyed geometric patterns using fermented mud and natural plant dyes.
