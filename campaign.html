MARKETING STRATEGY

THE CAMPAIGN

"WEAR YOUR STORY."

Our campaign celebrates identity, culture, and self-expression. We want young people to feel confident expressing who they are through fashion.

VISION

CAMPAIGN GOAL

Our goal is to introduce JOKER as a fashion brand that connects modern style with cultural inspiration, while encouraging more conscious choices instead of fast-fashion habits.

AUDIENCE PROFILE

TARGET AUDIENCE

Young people approximately 14–30 who:

  • Care about personal style and self-expression
  • Value individuality and authentic storytelling
  • Enjoy modern streetwear aesthetics
  • Appreciate cultural creativity and heritage
  • Want fashion with personal meaning
  • Are interested in responsible, slow fashion choices

They buy JOKER because the clothes combine contemporary streetwear with meaningful cultural inspiration.
